Clustering strength

Clustering strength is how many more percentage points of a group's voters ranked another of the group's candidates second than you'd expect if second choices followed popularity alone. 0 means the group adds nothing beyond its members being popular; below 0 means its voters ranked each other less than popularity predicts. These are associations, not effects: a strong grouping shows that voters treated the candidates as belonging together, not that an endorsement caused it.
