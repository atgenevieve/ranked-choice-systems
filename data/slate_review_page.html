Slate review

Is this the kind of grouping the model's slates should stand for?

The ballots already show that these voters ranked each other. The question is why, as far as anyone who knows the race can tell. Mark Counts when the candidates share a political grouping you'd recognize: shared politics, a coalition, a shared base or identity, of a kind that could exist in other elections. Mark Doesn't count when the likely reason is something else, such as neighborhood, name recognition, incumbency, or voters ranking the two front-runners. Mark Unsure when you can't tell. A rule of thumb: count it if you can name what the candidates have in common politically.

  1. Pick a place and year. Each is reviewed separately, so someone who knows that place's politics can do it.
  2. Set a rule for each type of grouping, such as unions or candidate pacts. Every row of that type follows the rule.
  3. Override single rows where you know better, and add a reason.
