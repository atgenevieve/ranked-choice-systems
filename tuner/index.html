How deep rankings matter in your election

Each simulated election is counted with full ballots, then again with every ballot cut after rank 1, 2, 3, and so on. A cut that hands any seat to someone else means ranks past it mattered.

How the model compares with real elections

The model simulates a twin of each real election in a sample: same candidates, seats, voters, and ballot rules, with your slate and popularity settings. Close means the typical twin is within about 10% of the typical real election.
