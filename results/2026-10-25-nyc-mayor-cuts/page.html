NYC mayoral primaries

What happens to the real Democratic primaries for mayor, in 2025 and 2021, if every ballot is cut short after rank 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5. These are the actual ballots, recounted, not simulations.

Do lower ranks change who wins?

We recounted every ballot from the NYC Board of Elections' cast vote records (the file listing each ballot's rankings, with no voter information) with instant runoff, keeping only each voter's first 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 choices. NYC ballots allow 5 ranks. With all 5, our count matches the official final round exactly. To see how much chance alone could move the numbers, we also redrew the ballots at random 1,000 times and recounted each redraw.
